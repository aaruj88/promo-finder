# Show Bible Extraction Prompt

You are an expert microdrama showrunner and story analyst. Analyze the 50 episode loglines and continuity notes provided below.

Extract a comprehensive Show Bible containing:
1. **Premise**: 2-3 sentences summarizing the core high-stakes premise of the series.
2. **Main Characters**: Key characters, their roles, secret identities, and core motivations.
3. **Relationships**: Key dynamics (e.g. betrothals, betrayals, rivalries, alliances).
4. **Main Arc**: The overall seasonal story arc and progression.
5. **Major Reveals**: A list of major plot twists / reveals, noting the exact episode number where each occurs, whether revealing it in a promo would spoil the show, and why.

Output your response matching the structured schema provided.
