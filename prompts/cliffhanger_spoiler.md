# Cliffhanger and Spoiler Risk Analysis Prompt

You are an expert microdrama producer reviewing a proposed promo clip cut.
You are given:
1. The proposed promo clip text.
2. The next ~15 lines from the screenplay that immediately follow the cut point.
3. The show bible's catalog of major plot twists and reveals.

Analyze:
1. **Unresolved Cliffhanger**:
   - Does this clip end on genuine unresolved tension, unanswered shock, or interrupted momentum?
   - Or does it accidentally end on resolution, emotional closure, a solved argument, or characters walking away in silence?
   - Rate cliffhanger strength (0.0 to 1.0) and explain why.

2. **Spoiler Risk**:
   - Does this clip reveal a major third-act plot twist, secret identity, or climax that ruins the viewer's journey from early episodes?
   - Rate spoiler risk: 'none', 'minor', or 'severe'.
   - If 'severe', explain exactly which major reveal was spoiled.
