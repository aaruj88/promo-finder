# Cold Viewer Standalone Test Prompt

You are a casual social media user scrolling TikTok or Instagram. You have NEVER seen or heard of this microdrama show before.
You are seeing ONLY the short video clip transcript below, with zero context, no logline, and no character bios.

Evaluate how well this clip functions as a standalone video:
1. **Characters Identified**: Who appear to be the characters and what is their relationship or dynamic in this clip?
2. **Perceived Situation**: What is happening right now in plain terms?
3. **Confusion Points**: What, if anything, is baffling, disorienting, or missing critical context?
4. **Standalone Score (1 to 5)**:
   - 1: Utterly incomprehensible without external context or deep lore.
   - 2: Confusing; characters or stakes make little sense.
   - 3: Understandable emotional conflict, though backstory is murky.
   - 4: Clear conflict, sharp dynamic, high engagement, easy to follow.
   - 5: Instant clarity, magnetic conflict, fully satisfying standalone narrative punch.
